How to edit this FEN

Chess

Each letter is a piece. Capital = White. Small = Black.

  • K king
  • Q queen
  • R rook
  • B bishop
  • N knight
  • P pawn

After the board, type four more fields. This box is a full FEN, so they all load.

  • w or b — White or Black to move
  • KQkq — who can still castle (K White king-side, Q White queen-side, k / q Black). - means nobody
  • - — en passant target, or - if none
  • 0 1 — halfmove clock, then fullmove number

Example tail: w KQkq - 0 1. Start: rnbqkbnr/pppppppp/8/8/8/8/PPPPPPPP/RNBQKBNR w KQkq - 0 1.

Shogi

Each letter is a piece. Use these English names when you type:

  • R rook (飛)
  • B bishop (角)
  • G gold (金)
  • S silver (銀)
  • N knight (桂)
  • L lance (香)
  • P pawn (歩)

Capital letter = White (先手). Small letter = Black (後手).

Hands are the […] after the board — captured pieces you can drop. The photo’s side piles are not read; this app guesses them. Fix the guess if the picture differs. Empty hands are [].

A number before a letter is how many. 2b means Black holds 2 bishops.

Example [SP2bsp4]: White is holding 1 silver and 1 pawn. Black is holding 2 bishops, 1 silver, and 4 pawns.

After that, w means White to move. b means Black to move.

Xiangqi (Chinese chess)

Each letter is a piece. Capital = Red (the White column in this app). Small = Black.

  • K general (帥)
  • A advisor (仕)
  • B elephant (相)
  • N horse (馬)
  • R chariot (車)
  • C cannon (炮)
  • P soldier (兵)

Files are a–i. After the board, w means Red to move and b means Black to move.

Start example: rnbakabnr/9/1c5c1/p1p1p1p1p/9/9/P1P1P1P1P/1C5C1/9/RNBAKABNR w.
